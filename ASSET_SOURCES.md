# Asset sources

This file records the provenance and intended use of non-code assets in the website.

## Portraits

Most portraits in `images/teampic/` were supplied by the lab or the people pictured and were already used by the previous Katsevich Lab website. The Jeffrey Zhang portrait comes from his [University of Chicago Data Science Institute profile](https://datascience.uchicago.edu/people/jeffrey-zhang/); the Jyotishka Ray Choudhury portrait comes from his [Georgia Tech ISyE profile](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/users/jyotishka-ray-choudhury). They are used only on the People page and related previews.

## Software marks

The hex marks in `images/softwarepic/` are project marks for sceptre, PerturbPlan, and ondisc. They were already maintained by the lab website and are used only to identify their respective projects.

## Social preview

`public/og.png` was generated for this redesign on July 21, 2026. It uses the site’s “calibrated signal” visual language and contains the exact site title, research positioning, and institution name.

## Favicon

`public/favicon.png` and `public/apple-touch-icon.png` are optimized sizes derived from the prior Katsevich Lab favicon.
